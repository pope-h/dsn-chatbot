# Data Science Nigeria (DSN) — knowledge base

> Compiled Oct 2026 from public web sources (DSN annual reports, TechCabal, BusinessDay,
> Techeconomy, DSN event pages). The official site blocked automated access, so details
> marked (unverified) or (dated) should be confirmed at https://datasciencenigeria.org.

## Who they are
- Data Science Nigeria (DSN) describes itself as Africa's foremost AI and Data Science
  organisation, focused on talent development and sector-specific AI solutions.
- Legally registered as the Data Scientists Network Foundation (non-profit). Also goes by
  "Data Scientists Network" / DSNai.
- Work areas: research, product development, training and community building, consulting,
  and incubating AI startups.
- Founder / CEO: Olubayo Adekanmbi (listed as Founder and Lead Mentor in 2023 materials).
- Mission: raise 1 million AI talents in Nigeria/Africa within ten years, and build AI
  solutions that improve the lives of 2 billion people in emerging markets.
- Website: https://datasciencenigeria.org

## Programmes and platforms
- **AI Bootcamp**: annual, all-expenses-paid, residential bootcamp on advanced machine
  learning and deep learning. Highly competitive, multi-stage selection.
- **AI Everyday**: year-round introductory track delivered as six-week intensive sprints,
  for complete beginners.
- **AI Invasion**: free, in-person beginner AI classes held in centres across Nigeria. Open
  to everyone; registration is required and gives an AI+ Membership ID. Topics include
  intro to machine learning, regression and classification, clustering and sampling.
  (Most detailed public info is from the 2021 edition, 19 Jul – 6 Aug 2021; check for current dates.)
- **AI+ for Kids and Teens**: communities for primary/junior secondary pupils (about ages
  7–11) and for junior/senior secondary students (about ages 12–18).
- **AI+ Clubs**: campus-based groups for undergraduates to build AI skills beyond their courses.
- **Learning communities / hubs**: physical communities in cities and on campuses (an older
  page cites 61). Older material mentions an AI hub in Yaba, Lagos, and communities in Jos,
  Ekpoma, Akure, Abeokuta and Ife. (dated: confirm which are active)
- **Data Scientist on Demand** (talent recruitment) and **AI Startup Lab**.
- **Consulting** services.

## AI Bootcamp history
- 3rd edition (2018): lead sponsor Microsoft Africa; focus on deep learning for financial
  inclusion using Microsoft Azure.
- 2019: grand finale in Lagos; backers included Microsoft, Bluechip, Terragon, Axa Mansard, Facebook.
- 2023: hosted at Covenant University, Ogun State, with Microsoft Nigeria. Path: 5-day free
  intro AI classes, a 50-day pre-bootcamp programme, a qualification hackathon (500+
  participants), about 104 seats won.
- 7th edition (2024): University of Lagos; 200 participants from 34 states, chosen from
  36,000+ applicants; 26 experts from institutions including Microsoft, Google DeepMind,
  ESRI, NVIDIA and Stanford; 38 independent learning hubs represented.
- 8th edition (2025): University of Lagos (Tayo Aderinokun Lecture Theatre), 20–24 Oct 2025.
  Theme: "AI for All – Democratizing Intelligence and Driving Impact." 32,000+ applications
  from the 36 states and FCT; about 150–200 finalists; official page cites 6 tracks, 30
  speakers and participants from 13 African countries. Microsoft sponsored awards and gave
  every participant a certification voucher. (Sources differ on exact finalist numbers.)
- Typical selection path: free city AI classes → ~30-day pre-study → qualification
  hackathon/project → assessment → residential bootcamp.
